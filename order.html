Shipping Options

Choose the right shipping lane

THG offers 3 main shipping lanes from China to USA. The right choice depends on your goods type, weight, and urgency.

✈️
Standard Air
6–12 BSD

Epacket – Best for small packages

✅ Weight: 0.1 kg – 30 kg per package
✅ USPS tracking to your door
✅ Tax already included (Yun Express lane)
✅ Best for: clothes, accessories, small electronics, home goods
✅ Price calculated per kg
💡 BSD = Business Days (Mon–Fri, excl. holidays)
🚀
Express Air
3–5 BSD

DHL / Express – Fastest option

✅ Fastest delivery to USA
✅ Ideal for urgent orders or high-value goods
✅ DHL / FedEx tracking – world-class reliability
⚠️ Higher cost per kg vs. standard air
✅ Best for: time-sensitive, lightweight, high-value items
💡 Contact THG for exact express quote
🚢
Sea Freight
20–25 BSD

Bulk Sea – Best price for heavy cargo

✅ Minimum: 12 kg per package
✅ Cheapest per-kg cost – ideal for bulk orders
✅ Price tiers: 12 kg+, 21 kg+, 71 kg+, 100 kg+
✅ Best for: furniture, machinery, large household items, business imports
⚠️ Longer transit – plan ahead
💡 Volume weight: L × W × H ÷ 6000

📐 How shipping cost is calculated

THG charges based on ACTUAL weight or VOLUME weight – whichever is GREATER

🇻🇳 Vietnam → USA
L × W × H
divided by
5,000
= Volume weight (kg)
🇨🇳 China → USA
L × W × H
divided by
6,000
= Volume weight (kg)
Example: Package is 0.9 kg actual but 1.1 kg volume → charged at 1.1 kg

FAQ

Your questions, answered

The most common questions from Vietnamese Americans using THG for the first time.

🛒
How do I start ordering through THG?
+
Simple! Just copy the product link from Taobao, 1688, or Pinduoduo and send it to us via Zalo (0987.683.175) or Facebook. THG will verify the supplier, quote an all-in price, and guide you through each step. No Mandarin required, no Chinese account needed.
⏱️
How long does shipping from China to USA take?
+
Delivery time depends on the shipping lane you choose: • Standard Air (Epacket): 6–12 Business Days – most popular, great value • Express Air (DHL/FedEx): 3–5 Business Days – fastest, for urgent orders • Sea Freight (Bulk): 20–25 Business Days – cheapest per kg, for heavy/large cargo BSD = Business Days (Mon–Fri only, excluding US holidays and force majeure events). Vs. routing through Vietnam, you save 3–4 extra weeks and $150–300 in unnecessary costs.
💳
How do I pay THG from the USA?
+
THG accepts all major international payment methods – optimized for customers living abroad: • PayPal (most common for US-based customers) • Pingpong / Payoneer / WorldFirst (international transfers) • VND bank transfer (if you have a Vietnamese bank account) • Zelle / Venmo (USD, for US customers) Payment process: 50% deposit when confirming the order, 50% balance when goods are ready to ship.
📦
What if my goods arrive defective or don't match the description?
+
THG's strongest commitment: • Step 1: All goods are inspected and video-recorded at the China warehouse before shipping • Step 2: If a defect is found BEFORE shipping, THG immediately works with the Chinese supplier to exchange or refund • Step 3: If goods are defective after arrival in the USA (due to transit), THG supports insurance claims and compensation You will NEVER have to contact the Chinese supplier directly – THG handles it all for you.
🛃
Will I have to pay US customs / import tax?
+
It depends on the shipping lane: • Epacket Yun Express: Tax is ALREADY INCLUDED in the shipping price. You pay nothing extra at delivery. • Standard Epacket: Sender (THG) handles tax declaration. Tax is calculated as a % of declared value. • Bulk Sea Freight: US customs determines import duty and collects it directly from the recipient (3–7 business days after arrival). THG will advise on the best lane for your specific goods to minimize or eliminate customs costs.
🏷️
Can THG find and compare products for me from multiple suppliers?
+
Absolutely! This is one of THG's most valuable services – and it's free: • Just describe what you want to buy (or send a reference photo) • THG searches Taobao, 1688, and Pinduoduo and compares at least 3–5 suppliers • We verify ratings, transaction history, and quality in Mandarin • We present you with a quoted comparison with pros/cons so you can decide This sourcing & consultation service is 100% free – fees only apply when you confirm an order.
