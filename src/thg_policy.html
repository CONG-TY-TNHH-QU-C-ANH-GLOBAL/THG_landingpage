  • Max compensation: USD 30/parcel.

Not compensated
  • Seller errors: damage, mis-delivery, quality issues, duplicate orders, non-compliant packaging.
  • Failed delivery due to incorrect address, refusal, not home, not collecting.
  • Transit damage (warehouse to delivery).
  • Delays — THG does not guarantee transit times.
  • Customs seizure due to infringement, prohibited goods, or under-declaration.
  • Force majeure events (war, natural disasters, pandemics, government actions, flight delays, etc.).
  • Fragile goods (ceramics, glass, specialty plastics) — ship at own risk.
If THG incurs penalties due to seller violations: customer bears HKD 1,160/parcel plus any additional losses.
📋Other Requirements
▼
  • Provide sales link and HS customs code to facilitate clearance.
  • Product name must be specific — not a broad category (e.g. "Men's cotton knitted sweatshirts", not just "clothing").
  • Multiple parcels to same recipient on same day: cumulative declared value must not exceed the country limit.
  • Recipient name must not contain company identifiers (GmbH, kft, SRL, Ltd) — may trigger B2B classification.
  • Saudi Arabia: Max 2 shipments/day per recipient; max 3 SKUs per shipment.
  • Fragile items: add shockproof material, bubble wrap, and fragile labels before shipping. THG does not compensate transit damage.
